How to Play

Movement: Arrow keys / WASD, or the on-screen D-pad. Walking into a monster attacks it.

Abilities: Press 1 / 2 / 3 (or click them in the sidebar) to use class abilities. Each costs a resource and has a cooldown measured in turns.

Space: wait a turn. Enter: use stairs / interact. Esc: pause menu.

Explore each level for gold, gear, potions, chests, and hidden traps. Find the stairs (>) to descend — every level is tougher than the last. Every 5th level holds a boss.

Shrines let you heal or bless yourself once. Wandering shopkeepers will trade gold for gear. Die, and your run ends — but your legend is recorded on the board.

Choose your Race, Class, and Background carefully at character creation — together they define your build, starting gear, and playstyle.
